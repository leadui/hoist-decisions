01
How it works

Pause. Decide. Record.

Hoist only steps in where a choice is hard to walk back. Everywhere else, your agent keeps its speed.

1 Pause

The agent asks before it commits you

A hook watches what the agent changes, not what it says. When it is about to add a database, or ask you something hard to undo, it offers to slow down first.

The database and the sign-in method are hard to change later. How do you want to decide?

Answer hereQuick: I'll ask you the questions now.Decide with HoistOpens a page that explains each option in plain words.
2 Decide

One canvas, shaped by the decision

Muse Spark drafts the options in plain words. Your answers become a matrix that shows what would flip the pick; the agent's lean, the risks and a critic sit beside it. This canvas is live: tap an option, or switch the kind of decision.

3 Record

The decision goes back to the agent, in writing

You commit; Hoist writes a MADR record to docs/decisions/ and the agent continues with your choice. Later, a change that conflicts with a record is blocked, and the record is cited.

status: accepted   door: one-way
deciders: project owner   confidence: medium

# Choose persistence for users and projects: SQLite

> Decision: SQLite. Because it keeps one file the app
  owns, with real queries. Accepted cost: one server,
  one writer. Revisit if you outgrow one machine.
02
What we believe

Fast is good. Blind yes is not.

Hoist has two modes, because some days you want to think it through and some days you need to ship. Neither one decides in silence.

  1. i
    It's okay to interrupt.A pause costs a minute. A wrong database costs weeks.
  2. ii
    It's okay to ask dumb questions.Every option comes with an everyday analogy, what living with it is like, and the terms explained.
  3. iii
    It's okay to take your time.Decide later, send it back to chat, or pick for now. The record says why, so changing it later is a plan, not a regret.
  4. iv
    It's not okay to say yes to everything.Speed is fine. Silence is not: every call is written down.

  • Any agent can log with hoist log
  • Records still block conflicting changes

03
Supports Bento MADR

Records you can read at a glance.

Every moment and record is drawn with Bento MADR, an open library that lays a decision record out as a bento box: one compartment for the problem, the evidence, the verdict, what follows and how you'll know it worked. Hoist picks the canvas from the kind of decision and adds its own compartments with the kit's define().

Open the catalog GitHub
Complete at a glance

White compartments are filled. Yellow is what a record still owes you — and the empty compartment says what to add.

04
Install

Three commands before your next database decision.

Hoist adds a hook, a skill and a docs/decisions/ folder to your project. The decision page runs on your machine.

$git clone https://github.com/leadui/hoist-decisions.git$python3 hoist-decisions/hoist.py install /path/to/project$python3 hoist-decisions/hoist.py serve /path/to/project # optional: let the agent decide, with every call recorded$python3 hoist-decisions/hoist.py mode autopilot /path/to/project
