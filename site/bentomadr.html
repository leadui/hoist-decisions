One decision, one canvas. A Bento MADR record is a grid of compartments — context, options, the verdict, what it costs, what could change it — drawn from the same data as the markdown record. Nothing is invented to fill a box: a compartment with no data hatches and tells you exactly what to add.

Complete at a glance

Every compartment reports present or missing. Grey means the data is there; yellow means the record still owes you something — readable from across the room, before you open a single section.
